Questions

What people usually ask first.

Does insurance cover this?

Often, yes. Many homeowners and landlord policies cover trauma, biohazard and sewage cleanup. We'll go over your policy with you and bill the carrier directly when it's covered.

Do I need to be there?

No. Many families prefer not to be. We can work from a key or lockbox and send photo updates so you can follow along from somewhere else.

What happens to belongings?

We sort with you into keep, clean, donate and dispose. Photos, documents and anything that looks personal are set aside for you to decide on.

When is it safe to go back in?

After verification. For drug residue that means surface testing. For biohazard and sewage it means cleaning, disinfection and drying are complete. We'll tell you plainly when it's ready.

How soon can you come?

Often the same day. On the first call we'll give you an exact time, and if it's better to wait a day for your sake, we'll say so.
