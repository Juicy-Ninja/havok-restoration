The clearance standard

We don't say clean.
We test it.

Five steps on every specialty job, in the same order, documented with photos and readings.

01 · Contain

Seal it off

Poly barriers and negative air keep it from spreading to the rest of the house.

02 · Remove

Take it out

Affected materials are bagged, manifested and disposed of under PA rules.

03 · Disinfect

Treat every surface

EPA-registered disinfectants, applied and given the full dwell time.

04 · Verify

Test it

ATP surface tests and odor checks. If it doesn't pass, we clean again.

05 · Report

Put it in writing

A clearance report with photos and readings, sent to you and your adjuster.

Before you call

Straight answers.

Does insurance cover this?
Often, depending on the cause and your policy. We check before we start and bill your carrier directly. If it isn't covered, you get a written price before any work begins.
Will the neighbors know?
Not from us. We arrive in unmarked vans on request and never post your address or photos of your home.
What happens to belongings?
Photos, documents, cash, jewelry and anything you name are set aside before anything is removed. Nothing is thrown out without your say.
Do I need to be there?
No. Many families live out of state. We work from a lockbox and send photo updates and the final report.
