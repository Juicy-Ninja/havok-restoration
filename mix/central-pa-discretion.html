What people ask first

Will the
neighbors know?

Will the neighbors know?
Not from us. Vans and uniforms carry no markings on these jobs. We can stage equipment out of sight and work at hours that suit you.
Does insurance cover this?
Often, depending on the cause and your policy. We check before we start and bill your carrier directly. If it isn't covered, you get a written price first.
What happens to belongings and valuables?
Photos, documents, cash, jewelry and anything you name are set aside before anything is removed. Nothing is discarded without your approval.
Do I have to be there?
No. Many families live out of state. We work from a lockbox and send photo updates and the final report.

Not ready to talk?

We'll call you.

Leave a first name and a number. That's all we need. Insurance details can wait.
