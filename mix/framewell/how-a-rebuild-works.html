01 · The hand-off

Nothing gets lost between companies.

Mitigation and reconstruction are two different contracts, and most homeowners get passed between two companies who never talk. When Havok dries your home, every moisture reading, photo, measurement and removed material goes into one ClaimTrak file.

Framewell opens that same file. Your project lead carries over. We start from the verified-dry readings, not a guess, and from the adjuster's estimate line by line.

You're never required to use Framewell after Havok. If you choose another contractor, we hand over the full mitigation file.

02 · Six phases

Built in layers. Inspected between.

    03 · Your schedule

    The same plan the crew works from.

    Your schedule lives in ClaimTrak. It updates as each trade finishes, inspections are marked, and if a date moves you get the reason the same day.

    04 · Living through it

    Stay home or move out?

    Many families stay. We set up dust containment, protect floors and paths, keep a working bathroom where we can, and leave a swept site every day.

    When the kitchen or only bathroom is out of service, your policy may cover additional living expenses. We'll talk it through at the walkthrough and give your adjuster the timeline they need.

    • Work hours agreed before we start
    • Containment with zip doors and negative air where needed
    • Kids, pets and work-from-home planned around
    • Daily photo update from your project lead

    05 · Walkthrough and keys

    Punch list, then keys.

    Final inspection first, then a walkthrough with you, room by room. Anything on the list gets fixed before we call the job done. Your warranty starts the day you sign off.

    You keep the job file: permits, inspection cards, photos and the drying record. It's the paperwork buyers ask for if you ever sell.
