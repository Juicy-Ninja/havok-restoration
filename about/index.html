Our story

Built in Johnstown to serve Central Pennsylvania

Havok Restoration of Central PA was started by people who grew up in the Conemaugh Valley and got tired of watching neighbors wait days for an out-of-town crew after a pipe burst or a fire.

We are part of the Havok Restoration family of locally owned franchises, which gives us national-grade training, equipment and insurance relationships. But the truck that shows up at your door is dispatched from Johnstown, and the person on the phone knows the difference between Westmont and Moxham.

Our technicians are trained and certified through the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC), the industry standard for water, fire, mold and biohazard work. We are a registered Pennsylvania home improvement contractor, fully licensed and insured, and we work with every major insurance carrier that writes policies in the region.

Most of all, we understand that you did not plan to meet us. Nobody calls a restoration company on a good day. So we show up ready to take the weight off your shoulders, explain everything in plain language, and stay until your home is yours again.
