Water damage is the most common property claim in Pennsylvania, and it is the one where the first day matters most. Water does not sit still. It wicks up drywall, soaks into subfloor, and pools inside wall cavities you cannot see. Within 24 to 48 hours, mold starts.

Here is what we tell every homeowner who calls our Johnstown line.

## Hour one: stop the water and make it safe

**Find the shutoff.** If the source is a supply line, appliance or water heater, turn off the main water valve. In most Johnstown-area homes it is where the line enters the basement, near the meter. If you cannot find it, we will walk you through it on the phone.

**Kill the power to wet areas.** If water is anywhere near outlets, appliances or your electrical panel, shut off those breakers. If the panel itself is wet or you would have to stand in water to reach it, do not touch it. Call the utility.

**Get people and pets out of contaminated water.** Sewage backups and outside floodwater are Category 3 water. Do not wade through it without boots and gloves, and keep children and pets out entirely.

## Hour two: call for help

Call a restoration company before you call your insurance carrier. This is not a sales line. Your homeowners policy almost certainly includes a duty to mitigate, which means you are required to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage. Getting professional drying started immediately is exactly what that clause is asking for.

When you call us at (814) 809-9833, we will ask:

- Where is the water coming from and is it stopped?
- Which rooms are affected, and which floor?
- Is the water clean (supply line), gray (dishwasher, washing machine) or black (sewage, floodwater)?
- Is the power safe?
- Have you contacted your insurance company yet?

Then we dispatch a crew. From Johnstown, most Cambria County calls see a truck within an hour.

## Hours two through four: document everything

Your adjuster will want proof of what happened and how bad it was. Before anything gets moved or thrown away:

1. **Photograph and video every affected room** from the doorway, then close-ups of damaged items and the water source.
2. **Photograph the water line** on walls, cabinets and furniture.
3. **Keep damaged items** until the adjuster or your restoration company has documented them. Do not toss the soaked rug yet.
4. **Write down the time** you discovered the damage and the time you shut off the water.

We do all of this professionally when we arrive, with moisture maps and thermal images, but your own photos from the first hour are valuable.

## What you can safely do while you wait

- Move furniture, electronics and valuables out of wet rooms or up onto blocks.
- Put aluminum foil or plastic under furniture legs that cannot be moved.
- Pull up wet area rugs and take them outside.
- Open cabinet doors and drawers in wet kitchens and bathrooms.
- Run the air conditioning in summer to pull humidity down.

## What not to do

- **Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.** It is an electrocution risk and the vacuum will be ruined.
- **Do not run box fans and call it dry.** Fans move air. They do not remove water from subfloor, drywall or framing. A wet wall cavity with a fan blowing on it is a mold farm.
- **Do not sign anything from a contractor who shows up uninvited.** Storm chasers work Central PA after every big weather event.
- **Do not wait for the adjuster to start drying.** Adjusters can take days. Mold takes 48 hours.
