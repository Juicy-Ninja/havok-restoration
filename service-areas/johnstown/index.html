We know Johnstown homes

Local knowledge matters when water is in the walls

Johnstown sits at the confluence of the Stonycreek and Little Conemaugh rivers, and the valley has a long memory of high water. Older housing stock in Cambria City, Moxham and Kernville means aging plumbing, slate roofs and stone foundations that take on water in spring thaw. Our crews are based here, so we are often on site in under an hour.

Every Havok crew carries the same equipment and follows the same IICRC protocols whether the job is in downtown Johnstown or a farmhouse ten miles out. What changes is the local knowledge: which carriers write most of the policies in Cambria County, which neighborhoods have combined sewers, and which streets flood first.

Restoration in Johnstown: your questions

How fast can you get to Johnstown?

We are based in Johnstown, so Johnstown calls are usually on site in under an hour, day or night.

Do you work with insurance companies in Cambria County?

Yes. We work with every major carrier that writes policies in Cambria County, including Erie, State Farm, Nationwide, Allstate and Penn National. We document the loss, submit the estimate and bill the carrier directly.

Do you handle both cleanup and rebuild in Johnstown?

Yes. Our mitigation crew stops the damage and dries or cleans the structure, then our in-house reconstruction team rebuilds. One company handles everything from the emergency call to the final walk-through.
