What to do in the first 30 minutes

Small actions now can save thousands later and make your insurance claim far smoother.

  1. Do not enter or attempt to clean the area yourself.
  2. Keep pets and family members away.
  3. If law enforcement is involved, wait for the scene to be released.
  4. Call us. We will explain exactly what happens next.

Biohazard & Trauma Cleanup: common questions

Is biohazard cleanup covered by insurance?

Most homeowners policies cover trauma scene and sewage cleanup. We bill the carrier directly whenever possible so the family does not have to manage it.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes. Our team holds IICRC and bloodborne pathogen training and follows OSHA and Pennsylvania DEP disposal regulations.
