Straight answers

Questions homeowners ask us

How quickly can you get to my home in Johnstown?

We answer the phone 24 hours a day and dispatch immediately. In Johnstown and the surrounding Cambria County boroughs we are typically on site within an hour. Altoona, Indiana, Greensburg and Somerset usually see a crew in 60 to 90 minutes.

Do I have to use the restoration company my insurance company recommends?

No. Pennsylvania homeowners have the right to choose their own contractor. Carriers may suggest a vendor from their program, but you are free to hire the company you trust. We work with every major carrier and bill them directly.

What does it cost? Do I pay anything up front?

Estimates are free. For covered losses, you typically pay only your deductible and we bill the rest to your insurance carrier. For uninsured losses we provide a written line-item estimate and offer financing options.

Do you do the rebuild too, or just the cleanup?

Both. Our mitigation crews stop the damage and dry or clean the structure, and our in-house reconstruction team rebuilds it. One company, one point of contact, from the first phone call to the final coat of paint.

Is Havok Restoration locally owned?

Yes. Havok Restoration of Central PA is a locally owned and operated business based in Johnstown. Our technicians live in the communities we serve.
