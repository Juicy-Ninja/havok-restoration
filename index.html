      Fair questions

      Straight answers.

      Plain talk about time, money and insurance. If yours isn't here, a dispatcher will answer it.

      How fast can you get here?
      We publish our live average response time, not a promise we can't keep. The clock starts when a dispatcher answers and is logged on every job, so you can see it on your tracker.
      Will insurance cover this?
      Sudden water, fire and storm damage is usually covered; slow leaks and upgrades often aren't. We document the loss with readings and photos from day one and show you which layer each cost belongs to before work starts. Your policy and your adjuster make the final call.
      What will I pay out of pocket?
      On a covered claim, usually your deductible plus anything your policy excludes. Every cost appears on your tracker with the reason it's there, and nothing new is added without your OK.
      How do I know it's actually dry?
      We measure it. Every room is read daily against a dry target, and you get final readings and a signed report before any rebuild starts.
      Who will I talk to during the job?
      One named project lead. Their name and number are on your tracker from day one. If they can't fix it, the owner will.
